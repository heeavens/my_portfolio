About Hola Bon

Our Story

Hola Bon was born out of a simple love for good food and good vibes. We believe that a warm, freshly baked roll can turn any ordinary moment into something worth remembering. That's what we set out to bring to the streets of Galway.

In 2026, we finally made the dream real — launching our food truck right here in the heart of Galway. We're brand new, full of energy, and our community of happy customers is growing every single day. This is just the beginning, and we're so excited to share every roll with you.

How We Bake

Every roll is made from scratch each morning in our food truck kitchen. We use premium ingredients — real Nutella, fresh fruit, quality cream cheese and carefully sourced local produce whenever possible.

Each batch is taste-tested before serving. We don't compromise on freshness or flavour — if it's not perfect, it doesn't make it to the window. That's the Hola Bon promise.
